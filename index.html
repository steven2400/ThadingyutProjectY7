WHAT IS THADINGYUT?

Thadingyut, also known as the Lighting Festival of Myanmar, is a major Buddhist festival marking the end of the Buddhist Lent and celebrating the Buddha’s descent from heaven.

Thadingyut is celebrated on the full moon day of the Burmese lunar month of Thadingyut, usually falling in October, and marks the conclusion of the three-month Buddhist Lent (Vassa) when monks traditionally remain in monasteries for study and meditation . The festival commemorates the Buddha’s return from the celestial realm after teaching the Abhidhamma to his mother, Maya, who had been reborn in heaven

Religious Significance

The festival honors the Buddha’s mother and expresses gratitude and reverence. According to tradition, when the Buddha descended from heaven, Sakra, the ruler of the Trayastrimsa Heaven, ordered stairways of gold, silver, and ruby to guide his path back to Earth Thadingyut symbolizes wisdom, enlightenment, and spiritual reflection.
